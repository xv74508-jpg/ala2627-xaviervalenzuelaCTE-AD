Fri 11 September · Day 27

The coherence pass, then ship

Read my own page as a stranger, fix what does not hold, and publish it.

Why this day exists

You have read this page forty times and can no longer see it. Today you get it back: read it cold, on a phone, as someone who has never met you.

What you do

  1. Write the taglineThe one line under your name. Hardest sentence on the page, which is why it is written last.
  2. Open it on your phoneNot a narrow browser window — your actual phone. Most people who open your link will be holding one.
  3. Four testsOne system. Reads on a phone. Movement has a reason. Nothing is a placeholder — no lorem ipsum, no “Project 1”, no dead links.
  4. Ship itCommit with your Agent: line, push, and check the live URL actually loads. Then turn that URL in.

Done when

It is live, it passes all four tests, and you would send the link to someone.
