App Design 2 · Week 7 · 8–11 September

The profile page

You already have This Is Me. This week you build the professional one — the front door of a portfolio that will hold everything you code this year. Not less creative. More deliberate: movement, style, and a page that holds together.

A four-day build
01–04
Write. Shape. Move. Ship.

The four days

Tue 8 Sep

The content, before any of the styling

Who you are, what you can build, what you have made, how to reach you. Written plain and true first — you cannot design your way out of having nothing to say.

Wed 9 Sep

Structure and type

One type scale, one spacing scale, clear sections. Coherence is a small number of decisions used everywhere, not a lot of decisions made once each.

Thu 10 Sep

Movement that earns its place

States, transitions, one considered reveal. Motion that helps someone read the page — and nothing that makes them wait.

Fri 11 Sep

The coherence pass, then ship

Read it as a stranger. Does it hold together, does it sound like you, would you send this link to someone who might hire you? Then publish.

The agent — five words

Start a message with one of these and the agent works that way for the reply. You are choosing how much of the thinking you keep.

DEBUG is the one to reach for. Finding a bug is the skill; typing the fix is not. That mode will help you narrow it down and will refuse to hand you the answer — on purpose, even if you ask it twice.

Your starter

Your own words, carried over

Everything you wrote on This Is Me is already in it. Not a blank file and not finished — the gap between it and the example is the assignment.

What makes it professional

Professional does not mean plain. It means every choice looks chosen. Four tests, and the page has to pass all four:

· One system. Two or three type sizes and three spacing values, used everywhere.
· It reads on a phone. Most people who open your link are holding one.
· Movement has a reason. Every animation either helps someone read or comes out.
· Nothing is a placeholder. No lorem ipsum, no “Project 1”, no dead links.

Say what the agent did

Every commit ends with one line
git commit -m "Add unit conversion helpers

Agent: debug"     # none · explain · review · debug · pair · spec
Not a gotcha — a record of how you worked, readable from the repo without anyone having to ask. By June you will be able to see the week you stopped needing PAIR.
