Tue 8 September · Day 24

The content, before any of the styling

Write what I actually have to say, before I decide how it looks.

Why this day exists

A profile page fails for one reason far more often than any other: there is nothing on it. Styling cannot rescue three sentences. Today you write the words — plainly, in a text file, with no CSS anywhere near you.

What you do

  1. Take stockOpen your This Is Me page and read it out loud. Which sentences are true and still sound like you, and which were written to fill a box?
  2. Answer four questions in plain textWho you are. What you can build. What you have actually made. How someone reaches you. One paragraph each, no formatting.
  3. Cut every sentence that says nothing“I am passionate about technology” is a sentence anyone could write. “I fix electronics and check whether they still work” is one only you would.
  4. Name the one thingIf a stranger remembers a single fact about you from this page, what should it be? Write it at the top — it becomes your tagline on Friday.

Done when

Four paragraphs of real content in a plain file. No styling has happened yet, on purpose.
